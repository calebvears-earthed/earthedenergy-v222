Questions we get asked

Straight answers.

How long does a system last?

The panels and racking are engineered for 25+ years. Inverters typically 10 to 15 years. Batteries 10 years (10-year warranty on GoodWe and Sigenergy stacks we install).

Will I still get the SA rebate?

Yes. Cheaper Home Batteries program is active for 2026. We calculate the correct tier for your kWh at quote time. No optimistic maths, no over-promising.

What’s the process?

Design consult → quote in 3 days → SAPN flex export application → install day → commissioning + handover. Typical timeline 3 to 5 weeks.
