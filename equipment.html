Panels

Trina by default. Astronergy when budget leads.

Trina Vertex S+ 475W (our default)

Glass-glass, 25-year performance warranty, low temperature coefficient (better in Adelaide summers), TOPCon cell tech. Best cost-per-watt on a premium panel today. Available through Tradezone + One Stop Warehouse (our two wholesalers).

Astronergy (budget alternative)

Where the customer wants the CHB rebate maximised and cost matters more than the last 2% of efficiency.

Optimisers · Tigo TS4-A-O

Per-panel optimisation on shaded roofs (chimneys, trees, aerials). Cheaper than microinverters, keeps the string architecture simple.

Inverters + battery stacks

Two stacks. Both whole-home.

GoodWe ESA All-in-One

Whole-home backup built into the inverter. Cleanest cost point for a fully-integrated hybrid. 10-year battery warranty.

Sigenergy SigenStor + Gateway

Modular. Zero-millisecond switchover. Full house at full load. Best when the customer wants no compromise on backup power or is planning V2H.

GoodWe Lynx (battery)

Modular LFP, stackable, matches ESA inverter. 5 kWh modules.

SigenStor BAT (battery)

Modular LFP, stackable, matches SigenStor controller. 9 kWh (8.76 usable) modules give best $/kWh right now.

Racking + BOS

The parts nobody asks about.

Clenergy tin-roof rails (default)

Powder-coated aluminium, wind + snow load rated to Adelaide zones.

BOS gear from Tradezone + MM Kent Town

DC isolators, breakers, labels, PV cable to AS/NZS 5033. All to CEC install guidelines.

Fit before brand

We don’t push a brand.

Every job starts with your roof, your usage, your budget, your backup priority. Then we spec the stack. If you want a brand we don’t currently install (Tesla Powerwall 3, Enphase microinverters), we’ll tell you upfront and either bring it in or route you to who does it best.
