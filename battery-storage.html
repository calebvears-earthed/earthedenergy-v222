Questions we get asked

Straight answers.

Will my whole house stay on during a blackout?

Yes, if you buy the right stack. We spec it explicitly. GoodWe ESA handles essentials cleanly (motor loads at ~30% derate). Sig + Gateway handles the whole house at full load with zero switchover delay. Both are whole-home. Neither is "essential circuits only". We don’t sell that half-solution.

Add a battery to my existing solar?

Often yes. We assess your existing inverter, switchboard, roof and cabling first. If your inverter is AC-coupled compatible, a Sig stack retrofits cleanly. If your inverter’s near end-of-life, we upgrade the inverter + battery together.

How much does whole-home backup actually cost extra?

On a Sig build, the Gateway adds ~$999 inc GST (1-phase) to $1,249 inc GST (3-phase). It’s not thousands. It’s the difference between a battery and a battery that actually keeps you running.
