What we wire

Three ways to do it.

7 kW single-phase (most homes)

Bolt-on to your existing 63A single-phase service. Solar-first charging when your panels are producing. Wallbox brands we install: Sigen EVAC 7, GoodWe HCA, Wallbox Pulsar, Tesla Wall Connector Gen 3.

11 to 22 kW three-phase

Bigger homes, workshops, dual EVs. Requires 3-phase supply. Faster charging, more parallel loads. Best paired with a Sigenergy stack for full load management. Brands: Sigen EVAC 11/22, GoodWe HCA 3-phase.

DC bi-directional (V2H · vehicle-to-home)

Only via Sigenergy SigenStor + EV DC Charging Module (12.5 kW, upgradable to 25 kW). Your car becomes a battery when the sun’s down. Solar → car → house. Real V2H, not a marketing brochure.

Questions we get asked

Straight answers.

Do I need to upgrade my switchboard?

Depends. Many Adelaide homes on 63A single-phase can add a 7kW wallbox without upgrade. Anything above 32A of new load usually triggers a switchboard review. We assess this on-site before quoting.

Can I charge only from solar?

Yes if you spec a wallbox with solar-first mode (Sigen, GoodWe, Wallbox Pulsar all support this). We configure it at commissioning.

How much does an install cost?

Wallbox hardware $900 to $2,200. Install $600 to $1,800 depending on cable run + switchboard work. Typical 7kW install lands $1,800 to $3,500 all in, inc GST.
