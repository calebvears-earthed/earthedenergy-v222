Questions we get asked

Straight answers.

Can the heat pump run off my solar?

Yes. A CT clamp watches your export and diverts surplus solar into the tank instead of selling it at 5c/kWh. It also works natively with GoodWe SEMS and SigenStor.

What size aircon do I need?

2.5 kW for a bedroom through to 9.4 kW for open-plan living. Multi-head runs up to 5 rooms off one outdoor unit. We size it on the room, the glazing and the roof orientation, not off a chart.

What does the heat pump cost?

Net ~$4,000 to $6,000 installed inc GST after the STC rebate, depending on tank size (250L for a family of 3 to 4, 315L for larger households) and the plumbing run.
